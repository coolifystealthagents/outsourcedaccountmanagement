# Design research: outsourced account management

## Search intent

Primary query: `outsourced account management services`

The closest useful organic category is outsourced customer success and managed customer support. Buyers are looking for a team that can own routine account follow-up, CRM hygiene, reporting, and escalation without handing over commercial decisions.

## Competitors inspected

### 1. SupportNinja

URL: https://www.supportninja.com/solutions/customer-success

Screenshot: `/home/hermes/.hermes/cache/screenshots/browser_screenshot_9e9ef23120d3464d9d85847972ec3ba9.png`

- Warm editorial look with serif headlines, coral actions, rounded workplace photos, and hand-drawn illustrations.
- The staggered image collage gives a people-led service some personality.
- Service details and proof sit too far down the page. Large blank areas made the inspected capture feel sparse.
- Useful pattern: pair human photography with small operational callouts rather than relying on generic office imagery alone.

### 2. PartnerHero

URL: https://www.partnerhero.com/

Screenshot: `/home/hermes/.hermes/cache/screenshots/browser_screenshot_a7ca7d6f2fc74efe9defc9e0505414fb.png`

- Contemporary enterprise system with a human-plus-software hero composition, clean sans serif type, dark operating modules, and bright orange actions.
- Service modes, capability cards, and a three-stage launch sequence make a complex managed service easier to understand.
- Several CTA labels compete with each other, and the page repeats transition branding heavily.
- Useful pattern: show the service as an operating system made of people, workflow, and reporting.

### 3. Helpware

URL: https://helpware.com/

Screenshot: `/home/hermes/.hermes/cache/screenshots/browser_screenshot_735f6ecf5ed64f36bd1affd02b43a00a.png`

- Premium dark corporate gateway with oversized editorial serif type and one accent color for each division.
- Art-directed human imagery makes the CX division feel more tangible.
- The page has little visible proof and no direct hero action. Several division panels spend too much space on atmosphere.
- Useful pattern: a neutral parent palette can support color-coded workstreams without making every module look unrelated.

### 4. SupportYourApp

URL: https://supportyourapp.com/

Screenshot: `/home/hermes/.hermes/cache/screenshots/browser_screenshot_001a27e7a90b4a80b5a812a211ab15a2.png`

- High-recognition yellow and black identity, rounded type, oversized metrics, compliance badges, and simple system diagrams.
- Security and procurement detail appear earlier than on most competitor pages.
- The inspected capture had severe blank-space and lazy-loading issues. CTA colors and labels also varied too much.
- Useful pattern: make operating controls visible near the top, especially for a service handling customer data.

### 5. HelpSquad

URL: https://helpsquad.com/customer-service-outsourcing/

Screenshot: `/home/hermes/.hermes/cache/screenshots/browser_screenshot_d2f9c2b0816b4263b16518016c16cb51.png`

- Strong two-column hero with editorial type, a human cutout, warm accent buttons, and compact buyer assurances.
- The page moves through people, channels, process, outcomes, fit, and FAQs in a clear buying sequence.
- Proof architecture is strong, but the capture showed large unloaded media areas and the page is too long.
- Useful pattern: put the person, the operating promise, and the first risk reducers in one view.

## Selected direction

**Winner: HelpSquad**

HelpSquad has the clearest visual hierarchy and the strongest service-buying sequence. The homepage will adapt that logic into an original account-management design, not copy its layout or branding.

The new direction is an **account portfolio desk**:

- Deep ink and warm paper palette with coral actions and cool mint status accents.
- Editorial serif display type paired with a direct sans serif body face.
- A local collaboration image framed beside an original CRM portfolio board. The photo shows coworkers at a shared desk; the nearby copy and original board supply the account-management context without claiming the subjects' roles or location.
- Account-status modules for renewals, replies, handoffs, and weekly review.
- Services shown as parts of the client-book workflow rather than generic outsourcing cards.
- One primary action throughout: `Request an account plan`.
- No public prices, customer logos, invented testimonials, ratings, savings claims, or unsupported performance numbers.

## Originality notes

The implementation uses no competitor copy, logos, illustrations, screenshots, branded UI, or proprietary assets. HelpSquad contributes only broad design logic: an editorial hierarchy, a visible human operator, compact risk reducers, and a clear service sequence. The portfolio board, copy, logo, component geometry, color system, and page composition are original to Outsourced Account Management.

The local hero image is stored at `public/account-team.jpg` and is used as a general collaboration scene. No claim is made about the subjects' nationality, employer, or profession. Staffing copy beside the image states that requests are limited to Filipino talent based in the Philippines.
