# Prepare a quarterly review with evidence

A review packet tied to decisions and metrics.

## Why this control matters

Prepare a quarterly review with evidence works when the record names a source, an owner, and a close condition before work moves.

## What to check first

Test one case by comparing the qbr preparation check record with the approved source and label any mismatch.

## Handle the exceptions

Log the exception with a reason and route it to the owner; do not fill a blank from memory.

## Review the evidence

Review the queue weekly and close only when the source, approval, and destination evidence agree.
