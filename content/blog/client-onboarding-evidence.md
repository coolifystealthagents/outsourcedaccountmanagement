# Keep evidence for client onboarding

A checklist of scope, contacts, and access.

## Why this control matters

Keep evidence for client onboarding works when the record names a source, an owner, and a close condition before work moves.

## What to check first

Test one case by comparing the client onboarding evidence record with the approved source and label any mismatch.

## Handle the exceptions

Log the exception with a reason and route it to the owner; do not fill a blank from memory.

## Review the evidence

Review the queue weekly and close only when the source, approval, and destination evidence agree.
