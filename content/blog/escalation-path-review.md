# Review the escalation path monthly

A test of triggers, owners, and response times.

## Why this control matters

Review the escalation path monthly works when the record names a source, an owner, and a close condition before work moves.

## What to check first

Test one case by comparing the escalation path review record with the approved source and label any mismatch.

## Handle the exceptions

Log the exception with a reason and route it to the owner; do not fill a blank from memory.

## Review the evidence

Review the queue weekly and close only when the source, approval, and destination evidence agree.
