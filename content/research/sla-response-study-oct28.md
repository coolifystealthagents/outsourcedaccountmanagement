# Are Service Commitments Met?

Response evidence against written commitments.

## Research question and scope

This protocol examines are service commitments met? in a defined operational record and does not claim an industry rate.

## Population and unit of analysis

The unit is one event at first observation; report the denominator and window beside every count.

## Evidence and data dictionary

Collect the source record and keep missing, unavailable, and confirmed negative as separate states.

## Limitations and next action

Present the result with its limits and set one review action for a later sample.
